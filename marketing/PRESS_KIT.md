## Description
Shmittens! is a cute rhythm shoot-'em-up where you **draw spells to the beat**. Shmitten the superhero
cat and her friends Pip, Nori and Kiwi fly from planet to planet to rescue the Princess of Pisitania
from Sho, the gloriously buff demon lord of the mice. Shoot on the beat, trace spells that land where you draw
them, and dodge boss spell cards whose bullets pulse with the music.

## Key facts
- Drawn spells (circle, line, zigzag, triangle), empowered on the beat and chained bar by bar.
- Boss spell cards with captures, beat armour and Spell Break.
- Companions who join your combo; the Catnip Overdrive; the rare PAWFRAME MK-IX mech.
- Relaxed / Normal / Hard / Bullet Hell modes, a full tutorial, Endless mode.
- All art, music and sound are generated in code: a distinctive pastel chiptune style.
