# Steam page copy (draft v1)

## Name
Shmittens!  (the ✰ lives in the logo only - it hurts store search)

## Short description (max 300 characters)
Draw spells to the beat! Circle a swarm, slash a row, zigzag lightning - all timed to the music.
A cute rhythm shoot-'em-up where one superhero cat takes on a very buff mouse lord and bullets that
dance to the song.

## About this game
**Sho, the buff demon lord of the mice, has kidnapped the Princess of Pisitania.**
Only Shmitten, the superhero cat, can bring her home, with her friends Pip, Nori and Kiwi.

**DRAW SPELLS TO THE BEAT**
Hold right-click and draw: circle a swarm to blast it, slash a line through a row, zigzag
lightning across a formation, or a triangle to shield yourself. Release on the beat to EMPOWER
your spell, and chain spells bar after bar to make the music sing.

**BULLETS THAT DANCE**
Every boss fights in named spell cards: blooming roses, cheese rain, crossing spirals. Their bullets
pulse with the music, so you can *hear* the next attack. Capture a card without being hit for
big rewards.

**PLAY ON THE BEAT, GET REWARDED**
PERFECT shots hit harder, build your combo and call your companions in: Pip zaps, Nori shields,
Kiwi shockwaves. Hit 50 in a row for the rainbow CATNIP OVERDRIVE. Play brilliantly and you might
even assemble the legendary **PAWFRAME MK-IX** mech suit.

**FOR EVERYONE**
- A step-by-step tutorial teaches every system.
- RELAXED mode, beginner assists and a guardian bubble.
- HARD and BULLET HELL modes for experts.
- Reduce-effects option and an adjustable beat offset.

**FEATURES**
- A planet-hopping journey from Pisitania to Sho's fortress world.
- Four drawn spells, three weapons (homing Fish, crit-happy Laser, bullet-eating Spread).
- Spell cards, graze, focus mode and beat armour for bullet-hell fans.
- Endless mode and a rank for every planet.
- Puns. So many puns. Also you can pet the cat.
