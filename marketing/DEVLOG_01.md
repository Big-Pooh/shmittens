# Devlog #1: Draw spells to the beat
Shmittens! started as a superhero-cat sprite and a question: what if a shmup's bombs were *drawings*,
and the whole game moved to the music?

Six playtests later, here's what we learned:
- **Clicking on the beat felt bad; Space felt great.** The mouse aims, Space shoots, and right-click draws.
- **Spells must land where you draw them.** Our first spells came from the cat and missed 3 times out of 4.
  Now a circle bursts where you circled, a line slashes along your line, and lightning strikes along your zigzag.
- **Bosses fight in spell cards, and their bullets pulse with the beat.** You can hear the next pattern coming.
- **Too many power-ups drown the core idea,** so the mech is now rare and drawn spells are the only thing that
  shatters boss armour.

Every sprite, song and meow is generated by code. Try the free web demo: https://big-pooh.github.io/shmittens/
