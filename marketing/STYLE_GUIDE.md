# Visual and audio style guide (procedural assets)
- **Palette:** pastel (lilac #D6CEF0, peach #FFD6B2, mint #B0ECD6, sky #AACDFF, gold #FFD65A) on a deep night sky (#0B0816). Every silhouette gets an ink outline #342248.
- **Characters:** chibi proportions (head ≈ 40% of height), big shiny eyes (2×3 px with a white highlight), a blush pixel, no sharp menace. Villains are funny, never scary.
- **Readability:** enemy bullets are hot pink / mint / lilac with a white core and a dark outline. Player shots are cyan / red / orange. Never use bullet colours for pickups.
- **Pixel scale:** sprites ×3 in play (bosses ×5–6). Nearest filtering, no rotation except rice bullets and the mech's idle wobble.
- **Audio:** chiptune in minor keys, four-on-the-floor kick so the beat is unmistakable. SFX stay short. Meows are sparse, tuned to the song's key, and −6 dB under the music.
- **Text:** puns and wholesome jokes. No real-world brands or franchise names.
