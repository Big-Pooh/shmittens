# Shmittens! ✰: player guide

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard / mouse |
|---|---|
| Move / focus (slow, shows your hitbox) | W A S D / hold Shift |
| Aim / shoot | mouse / Space (tap on the beat = PERFECT, hold = fire to the beat) |
| Weapons | 1 Spread · 2 Laser · 3 Fish (or Q / E, mouse wheel) |
| Draw a spell | hold right-click, draw over the enemies, release (on the beat = EMPOWERED) |
| Bomb / Overdrive | X (one per planet, needs 2 charges) / F (after 50 on-beat hits in a row) |
| Pause and settings | Esc (beat offset, volumes, meows, difficulty) |

## Spells (cost 1 charge; 10 cheese = 1 charge, max 3)
- **Circle:** a burst exactly where you circled; bigger circle, bigger blast.
- **Line:** a claw slash along your line, across the screen.
- **Zigzag:** lightning strikes every mouse near your stroke.
- **Triangle:** a shield bubble around you.
- Cast spells on consecutive bars to **chain** them (up to ×1.75). Any spell **shatters a boss's beat armour**.

## Bosses and spell cards
Bosses attack in named **spell cards**. Each card has a timer and a slice of the health bar. Empty the slice,
or survive the timer, to move on. Do it without getting hit or bombing to **CAPTURE** the card for a bonus,
a battery and, if a spell hit it, a **mech part**.

## Power-ups and helpers
- **Cheese:** spell fuel. **Batteries** and **yarn** come from on-beat streaks.
- **Companions** join every 10 combo: Pip zaps, Nori shields, Kiwi shockwaves.
- **Catnip Overdrive** (F): 5 s of a rainbow beam.
- **PAWFRAME MK-IX:** collect 4 mech parts (spell captures, 75-combos) for 12 s in the mech, with a beam rifle and beam saber.

## Difficulty
RELAXED (slower shots, extra life, guardian) · NORMAL · HARD (denser, faster, no help) ·
BULLET HELL (unlocked by beating HARD: twice the bullets, a bomb per life, graze ×3).
