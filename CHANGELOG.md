# Changelog
## 0.11.0 (in progress)
- Mech redesigned: PAWFRAME MK-IX (beam rifle + beam saber). Sparse, key-tuned meows (toggle in Settings).
- Spell roles: line slash 600 px; circle magnets cheese; zigzag breaks armour (x3 vs shield mice); boss cards show their weakness.
- Draw with the left button too (trackpads, browsers). Companions from planet 2, the mech from planet 3.
- BULLET HELL mode (renamed).

Full design history and the reasons behind each change: [`docs/AUDIT_LOG.md`](docs/AUDIT_LOG.md).

## 0.10.0 (first public build)
- Story of Pisitania and Sho; 4 planets (Mousetronica, Drainia, Ratopolis, Flexor) on a planet map.
- 4 bosses with bullet-hell spell cards (named attacks, timers, captures), beat armour and SPELL BREAK.
- Bullets pulse to the music; patterns follow the drums, the hi-hats and the melody.
- Drawn spells land where you draw them; snap assist; one bomb per planet.
- Weapons: Fish (homing), Laser (PERFECT = crit), Spread (point-blank, eats bullets).
- Companions Pip, Nori and Kiwi; Catnip Overdrive; the rare PAWFRAME MK-IX mech.
- Relaxed / Normal / Hard / Bullet Hell modes, Endless mode, tutorial with a spell-card practice boss.
- Gags, puns and a pet-able cat.
